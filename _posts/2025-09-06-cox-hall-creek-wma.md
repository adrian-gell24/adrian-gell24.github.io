<p>Cox Hall Creek Wildlife Management Area is a surprisingly peaceful retreat tucked away behind quiet Cape May neighborhoods. This was one of the first hiking and walking trail areas I explored during my time in the Greater Cape May area, and it left me with a memorable first impression. I would highly recommend it to others looking for either a peaceful and quiet community park or an easy 5km hike.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Directions:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Entering Cox Hall Creek WMA into Google Maps will take your drive through a cute neighborhood with different streets crossing into eachother from various different directions. The first time I drove the route I was confused, and thought to myself with all of these different roads, where is the wildlife management area? A lot of the places I have gone for hiking have a driveway populated with trees arching above your car, or lined with a meadow to the side off the shoulder of the road. However, there was no indication of nature surrounding this location. The neighborhood had a limited number of trees, and was filled with freshly paved roads, and beautiful homes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you are confused as well, stick with the guidance from Google Maps' voice. If you are taken down Delview Road, cross Bybrook Dr. onto an unmarked (but well-paved nonetheless) road with more densely planted trees up ahead. That road will take a turn to the right and turn into a sand and gravel covered road. You will also see a parking lot at the head of the trails. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You have reached your destination!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Cox Hall Creek WMA trails:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Upon exiting my car, I remember thinking to myself whether this is actually a hiking area. The first thing you see are well paved walking paths surrounding a pond stocked with trout. It reminded me more of some areas within Central Park in New York City than a hiking trail. Given that it is so well paved, I actually felt motivated  to have a jog. </p>
